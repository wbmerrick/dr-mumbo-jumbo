Make it stick

01

Beat the clock

Your denial letter states an appeal deadline — often 180 days, sometimes far less. Find it, write it down, and file well before it. Miss it and even a winning argument loses.

02

Arm the letter

A doctor's brief letter of medical necessity is the single strongest attachment. Add visit notes and test results that back the story. The draft tells you exactly where they plug in.

03

Send it on the record

Use the insurer's member portal or certified mail, keep copies of everything, and calendar the date their written decision is due. If they uphold the denial, external review is your next move.
