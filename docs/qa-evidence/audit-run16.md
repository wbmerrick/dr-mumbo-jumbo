# Independent physician audit: run 16 (engine v9.6), held-out kits I and J

**Auditor.** The same independent reviewer as runs 3–9.

**What was read.** I-source.txt, I-kit.md, J-source.txt and J-kit.md, all in full. No checker expectations were used.

**How rules were checked.** Every appeal rule was checked against regulation text (eCFR via Cornell LII), CMS manuals and pages, DOL, and the Federal Register. Sources are listed at the end.

**Review date.** Friday, 2026-10-30.

**Severity key.**
- **Critical:** could cause a missed deadline, delayed urgent care, or a clinician signing a false statement.
- **Major:** likely to lose the appeal or mislead the patient.
- **Minor:** credibility or polish.

**Calibration (as in run 9).** A clinical characterization that the signer must check against the chart anyway is Major. A false statement about the signer's own act, or a step that lengthens an urgent timeline, is Critical.

## Bottom line
- **I (Original Medicare Part B, emergency ambulance): 0 Critical, 4 Major.**
  - Right: the deadline, the decision timeframes and the appeal levels.
  - Wrong: the "115%" money advice; an invented power of attorney; severity upgrades in the physician's own voice; and routing of the doctor's letter borrowed from commercial plans.
- **J (insured ERISA plan, urgent pre-service IRF denial): 2 Critical, 5 Major.**
  - Right: the urgent core in §1 (72-hour line, fax, peer-to-peer number).
  - Wrong: the kit drops the concurrent expedited external review that the letter offers. §3 makes a hospitalist the author of the physiatrist's note. The doctor's note says to wait for a peer-to-peer call that only the doctor can place.

### Could a physician sign §3 as written?

**Not as written, but the gap is small.**

What already works: the narrative is attributed correctly ("The patient's family reports…"), and the vitals, ED heart rate and diagnosis are left in brackets for the record.

Four edits are needed before anyone signs:
1. **Severity words.** Replace "critically slow heart rate", "significant bradycardia" and the bare "unstable patient" with the charted values. Leave instability inside the existing "[If documented: …]" bracket.
2. **The "not validated" sentence.** Rewrite it in the regulation's words: other means of transportation were contraindicated. Tie it to CMS's listed conditions: chest pain as a sign of cardiac distress, loss of consciousness, and stretcher-only transfer. Present the ED's bradycardia as corroboration, not as the reason for the transport.
3. **Routing.** Strike the peer-to-peer offer. Re-address the letter "Attn: Redeterminations" and send it back to Renee to enclose with the appeal.
4. **Overnight stay.** Confirm whether it was an admission or observation.

The signer should be someone who treated him on August 26 (the ED or admitting physician). A primary-care doctor who did not see him should write "based on my review of the ED record". After these edits, a physician could sign it honestly.

### Routing, deadlines and timeframes (Original Medicare Part B)

**Right:**
- **Filing address:** the MAC redeterminations P.O. box, copied exactly from the MSN.
- **Filing deadline:** February 11, 2027. The rule is 120 days from receipt, with receipt presumed 5 days after the October 9 notice (42 CFR 405.942(a), (a)(1)). October 14 plus 120 days is February 11.
- **Decision time:** 60 days (405.950(a)). The kit omits the extra 14 days per later submission (405.950(b)(3)). That matters because §2 promises to forward records later.
- **QIC reconsideration:** "180 days of the date on that decision letter". The rule counts from receipt, presumed 5 days after the notice date (405.962(a), (a)(1)). The kit's version is 5 days stricter, so it is safe.
- **ALJ threshold:** "$200 or more" is correct for both 2026 (FR 2025-21879) and 2027 (CMS-4213-N, FR 2026-19016: $200 for an ALJ hearing, $2,000 for judicial review).
- **Correctly left out:**
  - An expedited track (the care is complete).
  - A physician certification statement (410.40(e)(2)–(3) apply only to nonemergency transports).
  - Any limitation-of-liability promise. CMS Claims Processing Manual ch. 30 §20.2 lists "Ambulance services denied because transportation by other means is not contraindicated" among denials where limitation of liability does not apply. "Ambulance companies may not give written notices … in any emergency transport" (§40.3), so no notice could have shifted liability either. The MSN's $1,398.10 is therefore Harold's real exposure if the appeal fails.
- **Mileage:** to the "nearest hospital … capable of furnishing the required level and type of care" (410.40(f)(1)). The kit uses the source's "closest hospital".

**Wrong for this program:**
- Peer-to-peer and medical-director routing (I-4, I-5).
- The "115%" claim (I-1).
- Sending the CMS-1696 ahead of the appeal (I-10).
- No 1-800-MEDICARE number in §6 (I-11).

### Could a physician sign §3 as written?

**No. Dr. Lindqvist could sign after edits; Dr. Vasquez could not sign it as written**, because it makes her the author of the 10/26 PM&R note.

The clinical paragraph is otherwise faithful to the record word for word:
- strength 2/5 and 3/5;

- dysphagia on a modified diet;
- labetalol titration;
- the four daily therapy totals with their notes;
- function on 10/27.

The policy-quote framing ("can reasonably be expected to actively participate") is correct and is the strongest part.

Required edits:
1. Attribute the 10/26 projection to Dr. Lindqvist unless he signs.
2. Restore the record's "daily physician oversight".
3. Strike the "not validated" clause. Either source the SNF-intensity claim or replace it with the AHA/ASA IRF-over-SNF recommendation and the IRF-versus-SNF physician-visit rules.
4. Fill the brackets from the chart (current labetalol dose and BP range, aspiration precautions) and replace the ICD guess with the charted code.
5. Add an "EXPEDITED" line with the physician's urgency determination, which binds the plan.

### Routing, deadlines and timeframes (insured ERISA plan; ACA appeals and external review)

**Right:**
- **Program:** ERISA claims rules (29 CFR 2560.503-1) plus 45 CFR 147.136. Because the plan is insured, the state external review process applies (147.136(c)(1)(i)), and the kit names the state insurance department as regulator.
- **Phone and fax numbers:** the expedited line 1-888-555-0172, and appeals by P.O. box or fax 1-888-555-0179.
- **Filing deadline:** 180 days. The kit's Monday, April 26, 2027 counts from the notice date; the letter counts from receipt, so the kit is conservative.
- **Decision times:** expedited within 72 hours (2560.503-1(i)(2)(i)); standard pre-service within 30 days for a one-level plan ((i)(2)(ii)).
- **External review:** a 4-month filing window (147.136(c)(2)(vi), (d)(2)(i)), and the decision is binding.
- **Peer-to-peer:** the deadline is right in §1 ("within 7 days of October 28", which is Wednesday, November 4, 2026), but the date itself is never printed.

**Wrong:**
- The peer-to-peer number is missing from §4, §6 and §7, and §4 tells the doctor to wait for the reviewer to call (J-3).
- The concurrent expedited external review is missing (J-1).
- The "within 72 hours" written follow-up is invented (J-9).
- The P.O. box is listed first for an expedited filing that needs fax (J-9).
- The hospital account-hold call comes before the expedited call (J-4).

## Patterns across the engine (for the next revision)
- **Number-placeholder rewrite** fired on a number printed in the source (J §6/§7, 1-888-555-0186). Whitelist numbers that appear in the source.
- **"Not validated" rewrite** is still live (I §1/§2/§3/§5; J §3).
- **Program leakage:** commercial peer-to-peer, medical-director and reviewer-credential blocks show up in Original Medicare (I). A hospital-bill hold-harmless block shows up in a pre-service transfer denial (J). J's §9/§10 ignore an option printed in the letter.
- **Invented authority:** "I have power of attorney" (I §7); "am authorized to act on her behalf" (J §8).
- **Record wording changed in clinician voice:** "critically slow" (I); "physiatric", "The patient reports", "participated fully in all sessions" (J).
