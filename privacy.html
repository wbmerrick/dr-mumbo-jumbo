Privacy

What we keep, and what we don't.

Short version: nothing you paste or upload is stored. Here is the long version, in plain words. Last updated October 30, 2026.

What happens to the text and files you submit

When you paste a note, bill or denial letter, or add a photo or PDF, it is sent once to the AI model (Anthropic's Claude, through its API) to produce your translation, appeal letter or Fight Pack, and the result is sent back to your browser. We do not store what you submit or what comes back, we do not log it, and we never see it. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API inputs and outputs are not used to train its models.

Because of that, we cannot recover a result for you later. Copy or print anything you want to keep.

What stays in your browser

The Fight Pack saves your finished kit in your own browser's storage for three days, so you can come back to it. It never leaves your device, and the "Clear it from this browser" link under the kit deletes it. The Fight Pack also keeps a short-lived unlock token in your browser so you can rebuild a kit without asking for a new one. We do not use cookies, analytics scripts or advertising trackers.

What we do keep

  • Waitlist sign-ups. If you leave your email for Fight Pack launch news, we keep the address and the category you chose, nothing else, in our database. We use it only for that news. Email us to have it deleted.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider (Vercel) keeps standard, short-lived request logs (time, URL, IP address, browser type) for security and operations, as every website host does. They do not contain the text you submit.
  • Fair-use counters. To keep the free tools free, each server counts requests per IP address for a day, in memory. The count is not written anywhere and disappears when the server restarts.

Payments

The Fight Pack is free at the moment. If paid options are introduced, payment will be handled by Stripe on Stripe's own pages; we will not see or store card numbers.

Leave out what isn't needed

You do not need to include names, dates of birth or ID numbers for the tools to work. Where a letter needs them, it leaves [brackets] for you to fill in privately. Please do not submit another person's records without their permission.

Children

The site is for adults handling their own, or a family member's, medical paperwork. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions, or a deletion request: hello@dr-mumbo-jumbo.com.
