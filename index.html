What this is (and isn't)

Dr Mumbo Jumbo IS…

  • A translator for the medical language in your documents
  • A decoder of abbreviations, lab-speak, and billing codes
  • A question-builder so visits with your real doctor go further
  • Free to use, no account needed

…and ISN'T

  • A doctor, a diagnosis, or a second opinion
  • Treatment advice — it never tells you to start, stop, or change anything
  • A place for emergencies

If something feels wrong right now, call your doctor — or 911 in an emergency.
