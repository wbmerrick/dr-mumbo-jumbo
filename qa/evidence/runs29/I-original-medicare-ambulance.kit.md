## 9. If they say no again — your next level of review

Original Medicare does not have external review. After a redetermination, if the contractor upholds the denial, the next level is a reconsideration by a Qualified Independent Contractor (QIC). You must request it within 180 days of receiving the redetermination decision. The QIC decides on the record, without a hearing — you do not appear or testify; the decision is based on the written evidence.

**To request reconsideration:**

Fill out the reconsideration request form the QIC sends with the redetermination decision, or write a letter that includes:
- Harold's name, Medicare number (4TN7-WQ2-HJ85), and the claim number (26-24318-774-120)
- The date of the redetermination decision you are appealing
- A brief statement: "I disagree with the redetermination decision dated [date] denying the ambulance claim for August 26, 2026. The documentation shows that other transportation would have endangered Harold's health given his syncope, chest pain, and bradycardia at the time."
- Your signature and contact information

**Enclose:**
- The redetermination decision letter
- Any additional documentation (if new records or a physician statement were not available for the redetermination, include them now)

Mail it to the QIC address on the redetermination decision within 180 days of receiving that decision.

The QIC generally decides within 60 days. If the amount in controversy meets the threshold stated in the QIC's decision and the QIC upholds the denial, the next level is a hearing before an Administrative Law Judge.

If you need help understanding your redetermination decision or the reconsideration process, call 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227) or the Tennessee SHIP at 1-877-555-0144.
