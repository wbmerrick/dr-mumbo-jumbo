**Before the call:**

**Criteria, point by point:**
The denial states home sleep apnea testing is appropriate for "uncomplicated patients," implying attended polysomnography is covered when the patient is complicated.

- **Heart failure documented:** Yes — [diagnosis, ejection fraction or NYHA class, current treatment with furosemide].
- **Why home testing is not appropriate for this patient:** Home sleep apnea testing is not validated in patients with significant cardiac disease. It does not monitor ECG or cardiopulmonary interactions that may affect treatment decisions in a heart failure patient. Attended polysomnography is recommended when cardiac comorbidities are present because it provides continuous monitoring of oxygen saturation, heart rhythm, and respiratory effort that home testing may miss.
- **Clinical necessity:** Sleep-disordered breathing can worsen heart failure and increases cardiovascular risk. Accurate diagnosis and characterization — including detection of central sleep apnea, Cheyne-Stokes respiration, and arrhythmias — requires the full monitoring available only in an attended study.

**If they push back:**

Repeating the test after an inadequate study delays diagnosis and appropriate treatment.
- *"The patient can be monitored as an outpatient":* Agreed — attended polysomnography is an outpatient procedure; we are requesting the attended study, not inpatient admission.

**The ask:**
Please overturn the denial and authorize attended polysomnography now. If you are not overturning it on this call, I need in writing the specific criterion you consider unmet, your specialty, and what additional documentation would change the decision.

## 10. Your deadlines and next moves
