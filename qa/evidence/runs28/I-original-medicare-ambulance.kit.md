## 9. If they say no again — your next level of review

If the Medicare contractor denies your redetermination, the decision letter will explain your right to request a reconsideration by a Qualified Independent Contractor (QIC). You must request reconsideration within 180 days from the date you receive the redetermination decision.

The reconsideration request goes to the address printed on that decision letter. Circle the denied service(s) on the decision, explain why you disagree, and enclose any additional records or information that support your case — the ambulance trip report, emergency department records, and the doctor's letter if you did not already send them, or any records from the overnight hospital stay that further document the cardiac emergency and the medical need for monitored ambulance transport.

**If you want to send additional evidence to the QIC after filing:** You can mail records to the address on the reconsideration decision with your father's name, Medicare Number (4TN7-WQ2-HJ85), and the claim number (26-24318-774-120) on every page. Each submission you send after the initial request can add up to 14 days to the decision time.

The QIC decides on the record — there is no hearing at this level. If the QIC upholds the denial and the amount in controversy meets the threshold stated in that decision, you can request a hearing before an Administrative Law Judge; the QIC's decision letter will explain that next level and the deadline.

Keep copies of everything you send at every level.
