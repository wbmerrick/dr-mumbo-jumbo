**Criteria, point by point:**
The denial stated home testing is appropriate for "uncomplicated patients." This patient is not uncomplicated: congestive heart failure [NYHA class or ejection fraction if documented] substantially increases the risk of central sleep apnea, which a home test is not validated to detect reliably. The patient's symptoms — witnessed apneas, loud snoring, and severe sleepiness [Epworth Sleepiness Scale score if documented] — meet the clinical threshold for sleep-disordered breathing evaluation, and the cardiopulmonary comorbidity requires attended polysomnography per [cite: the insurer's own policy criteria or the relevant specialty-society guideline].

**If they push back:**
- *"Try the home test first — if it's negative or inconclusive, we can approve the in-lab study then."* The home test is not validated in patients with heart failure and is expected to be ineffective for detecting central apneas; a negative result would still require the in-lab study, delaying appropriate treatment for a condition contributing to cardiac decompensation.
- *"The clinical notes don't document severity."* [If applicable:] The patient is on loop diuretic therapy for heart failure, reports falling asleep while driving, and has witnessed apneas — these findings establish both the cardiac risk and the severity of sleepiness. I can provide an updated note documenting [ejection fraction, NYHA class, sleepiness score] if that would satisfy the requirement.
- *"We need conservative treatment documented first."* Sleep-disordered breathing evaluation is the conservative diagnostic step before initiating therapy; there is no less invasive way to diagnose the type of sleep apnea in a patient with heart failure, and the diagnosis will guide whether CPAP, adaptive servoventilation, or another therapy is appropriate.

**The ask:**
Overturn the denial now and approve attended polysomnography. If you are not overturning it on this call, please provide in writing the specific criterion you consider unmet, your specialty, and what documentation would change the decision.

**After the call:**
