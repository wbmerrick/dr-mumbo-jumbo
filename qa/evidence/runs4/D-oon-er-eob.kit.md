**Case strength:** Strong. The presenting symptoms (crushing chest pain, shortness of breath) meet the prudent-layperson emergency standard, the facility was participating, and federal law protects you from balance billing by the out-of-network ER clinician.

- Confirmation that Mercy Lakeside Hospital is a participating facility in your plan
