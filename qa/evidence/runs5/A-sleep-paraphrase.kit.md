## 10. Your deadlines and next moves

- **File your internal appeal by [your appeal deadline — on your denial letter]** — for employer plans the general rule is 180 days from the notice date (the date on the letter), but use the deadline your letter prints. Send the patient's appeal letter (section 2), the doctor's letter (section 3), and the clinical records showing your heart failure diagnosis and sleep symptoms to the address on your denial letter.
- **The insurer must decide within 30 days** of receiving your appeal (or 15 days per level if your plan has two appeal levels) — this is the longest the plan may take.
- **If the internal appeal is denied,** you have the right to independent external review. Request it within the timeframe stated on the final denial letter (generally 4 months). The external review organization decides within 45 days, and that decision is binding on the plan.
- **Your next move after filing:** If you don't hear back within the decision timeframe, call the number on your denial letter to check the status. Keep copies of everything you send and note the date you mailed or submitted it.
- Self-funded versus fully insured plans follow slightly different rules — ask HR or check your plan document to confirm which applies, especially before filing a complaint.
