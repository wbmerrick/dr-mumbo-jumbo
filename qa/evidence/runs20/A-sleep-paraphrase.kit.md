**Do this first:** Get the exact policy criteria from the plan (call and request the medical policy or coverage criteria for polysomnography), and ask your cardiologist's or primary doctor's office to write a letter for your appeal documenting your heart failure and explaining why home sleep testing is not appropriate for you.
