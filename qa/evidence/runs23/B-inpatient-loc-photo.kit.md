**Case strength:** Good — one thing missing. The hospital coded an MCC, but the plan's reviewer says the documentation does not show the complications that would require inpatient care. Before asking the plan to overturn the decision, ask Member Services what your cost-sharing would be at each level — the difference may be small or may favor observation.
