Summit Anesthesia Partners may not balance bill you for the difference between the billed and allowed amounts ($1,860), and the plan must reprocess the claim at the in-network level.

**Case strength:** Strong. Federal surprise-billing protections apply to ancillary anesthesia services at in-network facilities, the facts fit squarely (participating surgery center, no advance notice or choice of anesthesiologist), and this is a processing error the plan can correct.

**Who fixes this:** You file the appeal asking the plan to reprocess the claim under federal surprise-billing rules at in-network cost-sharing.
