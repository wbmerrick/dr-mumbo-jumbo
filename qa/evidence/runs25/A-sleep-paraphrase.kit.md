## 10. Your deadlines and next moves

- **Appeal deadline:** [your appeal deadline — on your denial letter]. For employer plans, internal appeals generally must be filed within 180 days of the denial notice. The deadline printed on your letter controls — if anyone gives you a different date by phone, go by the earlier one.
