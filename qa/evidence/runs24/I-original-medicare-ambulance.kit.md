## 9. If they say no again — your next level of review

Original Medicare has no external review. If the Medicare contractor's redetermination says no, the next level is a reconsideration by a Qualified Independent Contractor (QIC). You must request it within 180 days of receiving the redetermination decision. The QIC decides on the record, without a hearing. The redetermination decision letter will explain how to request reconsideration and will give the address and deadline.

If the QIC also denies the claim, and the amount in controversy meets the threshold stated on that decision letter, you can request a hearing before an Administrative Law Judge. Each decision letter explains the next level and the deadline.

If you want to add evidence after the redetermination is filed — for example if the ambulance company or hospital sends records late, or if the doctor writes a letter after you've already mailed the appeal — call 1-800-MEDICARE at 1-800-633-4227 (TTY 1-877-486-2048) and ask how to submit additional documentation to the redetermination already in process. Do not wait for the decision and then start over — get the new evidence to the contractor while the redetermination is still open.

Keep copies of everything you send, and keep every decision letter you receive — each one starts the clock for the next level.

For questions about your father's Medicare coverage or the appeals process, call 1-800-MEDICARE or the Tennessee State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP) at 1-877-555-0144 for free, unbiased counseling.
