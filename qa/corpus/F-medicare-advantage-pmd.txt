DECISION: We have DENIED the medical item requested.

WHY WE DENIED: Our plan follows Medicare coverage rules for mobility assistive equipment (National Coverage Determination 280.3 and the Local Coverage Determination for Power Mobility Devices). To be covered, your medical records must show that you have a mobility limitation that significantly impairs your ability to perform one or more mobility-related activities of daily living (MRADLs) such as toileting, feeding, dressing, grooming, and bathing in your home; that the limitation cannot be sufficiently resolved by a cane or walker; that you do not have sufficient upper-extremity function to propel an optimally configured manual wheelchair; that you can safely operate a power wheelchair; and that a face-to-face mobility examination was completed. The face-to-face visit note dated 09/26/2026 documents chronic obstructive pulmonary disease and osteoarthritis of both knees, but states that you "ambulate with a rolling walker in the home" and does not describe why a walker or a manual wheelchair is insufficient for your MRADLs. The supplier's home assessment was not included with the request.
