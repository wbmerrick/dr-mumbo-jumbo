DECISION: NOT APPROVED.

Reason for decision: Harborline's Step Therapy policy for TNF inhibitors (Policy RX-ST-114) requires documentation of an inadequate response, intolerance, or contraindication to at least ONE conventional immunomodulator (azathioprine, 6-mercaptopurine, or methotrexate) for a minimum of 12 weeks before a biologic can be approved for moderate to severe Crohn's disease. The documentation submitted indicates treatment with mesalamine and two courses of oral corticosteroids, but does not document a trial of a conventional immunomodulator. In addition, the request did not include a recent colonoscopy or imaging report documenting current disease activity.

Covered alternatives: azathioprine, 6-mercaptopurine, methotrexate. Once step therapy criteria are met, the biosimilar adalimumab products Hadlima and Hyrimoz are preferred over Humira on your formulary.
