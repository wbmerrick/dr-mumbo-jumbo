What Dr Mumbo Jumbo is

Dr Mumbo Jumbo is an educational tool. It translates medical and insurance paperwork into plain English and drafts documents you can use to question a bill or appeal a denial. It was built by a practicing physician, but using it does not make that physician, or anyone else, your doctor.

What it is not

  • Not medical advice. A translation of your note is not a diagnosis or a treatment plan. Your care team makes the calls. If something you read sounds urgent, call them today; in an emergency, call 911.
  • Not legal or financial advice. The appeal letter and the Fight Pack are drafts for you, and where relevant your clinician, to review, complete and send yourselves. We do not file anything on your behalf, we are not your representative, and we make no promise about the outcome of any appeal.
  • Not a doctor–patient relationship. Nothing on the site creates one.

The drafts can be wrong

The translations and drafts are written by an AI model. It can misread a photo, mis-state a rule or invent a detail. Deadlines, plan rules and appeal rights vary by plan and state. Your denial letter and your plan documents control. Read every line before you send anything, check every number against your own paperwork, and fill in every [bracket] yourself.

Using the site fairly

  • Use it for your own paperwork, or for a family member or friend who has asked for your help.
  • Do not submit another person's records without their permission.
  • The tools are free and rate-limited so they stay free. Do not script, scrape or bulk-run them.
  • You are responsible for what you do with the documents it produces.
